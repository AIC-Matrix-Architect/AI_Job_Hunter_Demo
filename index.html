Full gamification architecture

The gamification system transforms the job hunt from a demoralising slog into a progression engine with visible rewards, daily re-engagement prompts, and cross-role incentive alignment. Every meaningful platform action can move a job seeker forward through Job Points (JP), credits, streaks, badges, leaderboards, and quest chains.

JP (Job Points) is the core progression metric. Every platform action can award JP, converting effort into visible progress.

Design insight: weekly points reset every Monday, keeping competition fresh and preventing new users from feeling permanently behind. A Friday warning creates urgency without actual demotion.

Credits buy auto-apply runs. They are earned through engagement, creating a closed loop: the more a job seeker engages, the more automation fuel becomes available.

Key idea: automation is a reward for engagement, not a free detachment from the process. The system says: “you did the hard work of engaging; now let me handle the grind.”

The system creates a mutual investment chain across seekers, heroes, and enterprises. Seeker progression can reward linked heroes, and hero progression can reward linked enterprises, creating accountability and aspirational modelling.
